# The uncomfortable paragraph

Spent the morning trying to write the status page without it reading as an
apology, and the honest version is shorter than every draft that tries to soften
it.

Nothing is deployed. No contracts, no live API, no members, nobody has posted
collateral because there is nowhere to post it. The specification is complete,
the arithmetic is tested and runnable today, and that is the whole inventory.

The temptation is to fill the gap with a roadmap. Roadmaps are free to write and
they convert an absence into a promise, which is worse than the absence: the
absence is checkable and the promise is not.

What we can put next to it is the work that does exist and is verifiable now. The
netting and waterfall arithmetic as a package with tests. Interfaces published
ahead of the implementation, which is the part actually worth arguing about. A
checks repository that runs against our own claims and has already contradicted
one of them in public.

A clearing layer asking to be trusted with margin should be legible before it is
live. Being blunt about what does not exist is the cheapest part of that, and it
is the part most projects skip.
