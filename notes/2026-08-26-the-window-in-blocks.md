# How many blocks are in a window

Writing the confirmation-depth section and reached for the two second block time
from May. Then thought about how that number got into the table: I wrote it down
in a note during the first week, and it has been repeated in three files since
without anybody measuring it.

Wrote a check instead of a paragraph. Head block, a block a million back, divide.

It is 0.101 seconds. Twenty times faster than the table says.

Nothing about netting or margin changes, because windows are wall clock and that
argument was made deliberately back in May. What changes is the block count
inside a window: about 2,959, not 150, and any confirmation depth sized off the
old figure is off by the same factor.

Two things to fix and one to keep. Fix the parameter table and fix the finality
assumption. Keep the note from 26 May exactly as written, because it is the piece
of evidence that the assumption was recorded rather than smuggled in, and it
carries the date it entered the table.

The check now runs on a schedule. The failure was not the wrong number, it was
having a number in the table that nothing was watching.
